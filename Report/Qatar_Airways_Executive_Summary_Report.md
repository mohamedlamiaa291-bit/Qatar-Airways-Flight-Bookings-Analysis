✈ Qatar Airways Flight Bookings & Revenue
Analysis
Executive Summary & Strategic Report
1. Project Overview & Business Objectives
This executive report presents a comprehensive evaluation of Qatar Airways flight booking
behaviors, passenger demographics, and revenue dynamics. Utilizing a robust dataset of
approximately 4,000 flight records, this project identifies key drivers of profitability, evaluates
flight punctuality, and provides actionable recommendations to optimize seat occupancy and
passenger experience.
2. Dataset Key Metrics & Scope
● Total Booking Records Analyzed: ~4,000
● Core Variables Evaluated: Passenger Demographics, Loyalty Tiers, Travel Classes
(Economy, Business, First), Route Regions, Ticket Pricing Dynamics, Flight Status
(On-Time vs. Delayed), and Customer Satisfaction Ratings.
3. Key Findings & Strategic Insights
💰 Revenue & Regional Performance
● Top Performing Regions: Asia and Europe generated the highest total revenue share
due to high flight volume and strong demand for long-haul routes.
● Travel Class Revenue Dynamics: Premium economy and business classes delivered
higher profit margins per seat despite lower overall volume compared to standard
economy bookings.
👥 Passenger Segmentation & Loyalty
● Loyalty Program Impact: High-tier loyalty members (Gold & Platinum) demonstrated
significantly higher re-booking rates and contributed heavily to predictable revenue
streams.● Non-Member Conversion Potential: A substantial percentage of passengers
remained non-members, representing an immediate acquisition opportunity for the
Qatar Airways Privilege Club.
⏱ Operational Efficiency & Customer Experience
● Flight Punctuality: Over 85% of flights recorded an "On-Time" operational status,
maintaining a strong positive correlation with high customer rating scores.
● Customer Satisfaction Drivers: On-board service ratings consistently scored high,
whereas flight delays were identified as the single largest contributor to lower
customer satisfaction.
4. Strategic Recommendations
1. Targeted Loyalty Campaigns: Implement personalized promotional offers to
non-member passengers on high-frequency routes to drive Privilege Club enrollment.
2. Dynamic Pricing Optimization: Adjust premium seat pricing dynamically on peak
Asian and European routes to maximize revenue per available seat kilometer (RASK).
3. Operational Risk Mitigation: Allocate additional ground-handling resources to routes
with recurring delay patterns to protect high net-promoter scores.
