## 🎯 Business Goal & Project Context
The primary objective of this project is to analyze flight booking behaviors, seat occupancy trends, and revenue patterns for **Qatar Airways**. By transforming raw booking logs into actionable insights, this analysis aims to optimize pricing strategies, minimize flight cancellations, and maximize average revenue per passenger across key routes.

This repository contains a comprehensive data analysis project focusing on **Qatar Airways flight bookings**. The dataset tracks passenger trends, ticket pricing dynamics, travel classes, loyalty tiers, customer satisfaction ratings, and on-time flight operational performance.

## 🎯 What I Analyzed
* Flight Booking Behaviors & Seat Occupancy Rates
* Regional Revenue Distribution & Ticket Pricing Dynamics
* Operational Punctuality & Aircraft Delays (Airbus A350 vs. Boeing 777)
* Passenger Segmentation & Loyalty Tier Value (Privilege Club)
* Booking Channel Conversion Rates (App, Website, Call Center)
* Relationship between Flight Delays, Service Quality & Customer Ratings

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

🟢 **> 85%** of flights were recorded as **On-Time** operational status.

⭐ Top performing routes achieved strong average customer rating scores.

🏷️ The dataset covers **3 primary travel classes** with distinct profit margins.

🌐 Long-haul routes in **Asia & Europe** generated the highest overall total revenue share.

**Revenue & Profitability Performance:**
`Economy Class` → `High Booking Volume / Standard Margins`

`Premium Economy` → `Moderate Volume / Higher Margin per Seat`

`Business & First Class` → `Max Margin per Seat / Strategic Long-Haul Revenue`

> These findings represent observed trends within the booking dataset and serve as a baseline for dynamic seat pricing optimization.

---

## ❓ Business & Research Questions
To achieve the project objectives, this analysis addresses the following core questions:
1. **Revenue Optimization:** Which travel classes and flight regions generate the highest average ticket price and revenue?
2. **Operational Efficiency:** How do flight delays (in minutes) and on-time performance vary across different aircraft types (e.g., Airbus A350 vs. Boeing 777)?
3. **Customer Satisfaction:** What is the relationship between booking channels (App, Website, Call Center), loyalty tiers, and customer satisfaction ratings?
4. **Flight Status Patterns:** What key factors influence flight delays and overall status performance?
